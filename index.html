🎓 How to Play

📜 Rules

Goal: Capture the enemy leader, or move your leader onto their throne (2-player).


Turn: Click a card → click a piece → click a highlighted cell to move. Used card enters the rotation queue.


Resource tiles (🔥💧🌿💨) — At your turn start, each piece that was already on a resource tile last turn earns 1 token. Two pieces on two same-element tiles = 2 tokens.


Element Tiers — unlock with 5 / 10 / 17 tokens of an element. Higher tiers include all lower ones. Unlocked tiers let you use the matching coloured move spaces shown on each card.


Card element moves — Cards show extra coloured dots in the move grid. Only you can use them if you have the required tier. The card header shows e.g. "💧1 🔥2" meaning it has Water tier-1 and Fire tier-2 extra spaces.


Leader abilities — Move your leader into the central 3×3 golden zone to activate a one-time ability:

🔴 Surge — move 2 pieces per turn for 3 turns.

🟢 Summon — instantly spawn a new pawn adjacent to your leader.

🔵 Warden's Shield — for 3 turns, pieces on resource tiles begin each turn shielded.

🟡 Leap — your leader may move twice per turn for 3 turns.
